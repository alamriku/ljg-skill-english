# ljg-paper research record

Records the grounding in the source, the selection of evidence and the reading-check results. It is not an outline for the body. Where there is no evidence, write "not found" and say what that affects; for an item that does not apply, give the reason rather than guessing. In entries separated by `|`, do not reuse that separator inside a field value.

## Source and scope of material

- Format version: 3
- Location in the source: {the single original URL, PDF or local path, the same location as the note's #+source}
- Paper information: {paper ID, original title, authors, version or date}
- Source hash: {SHA-256 of the PDF, local file or source package; if unavailable, say why}
- What the material supports: {which judgments it supports, which questions were not validated}
- External-instruction scan: {none found / stopped; describe any suspicious instruction and how it was handled}

## Research question and contribution

- Contribution type: {method/intervention, explanation/theory, measurement/benchmark, resource/system; for a mixed paper, state the main type}
- Object of study: {the specific object, phenomenon or relation}
- What existing methods lack: {what the prior method, explanation or benchmark cannot solve}
- Main contribution: {what the new method, finding, theory, benchmark, resource or system actually adds}
- Input, processing and output: {the key steps of the method, or the acting relations between factors; where input/output does not apply, describe the argumentative relation}
- Research questions or threads that cannot be dropped: {list them, separated by |}
- Why the threads belong to one paper: {how they jointly answer the question or form the contribution}
- Evidential support per thread: {for each thread: supported / partly supported / not yet supported, and on what grounds, separated by |}
- Claims the paper does not make: {specific reminders that prevent overreach}
- Paper-specific statements in the body: {at least two sentences or phrases that point unmistakably at the paper's object or contribution, separated by |}
- Question and contribution statements in the first two sections: {sentences or phrases that carry the research question and the main contribution, at least two, separated by |}
- Paper in brief: {problem, contribution, method or relation, key evidence, limits}
- How it differs from other papers: {what makes this explanation impossible to transplant onto another paper}

## Evidence record

Each entry gives the location, the result, the judgment it supports and the reason for keeping or cutting it. Roles are "core", "supporting" and "reference"; decisions are "into the body", "merged" and "reference only". At least one core entry; add the rest as needed - there is no requirement to fill every category. Entries that go into the body or are merged must have a matching statement findable in the body. Reference entries need no body statement.

- [evidence] Name: | Role: core | Location: | Direct result in the paper: | Judgment supported: | Matching statement in the body: | Decision: into the body | Reason:

## Independent reading-check record

Record the revision and the recheck; where nothing was found, say so honestly. Keep a user's reading difficulty on record - an old PASS does not carry over}
