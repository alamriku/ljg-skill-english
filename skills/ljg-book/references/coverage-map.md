# ljg-book material and check record

This is a research and verification record, not an outline for the body. The field names before the colon are read by the script, so keep their spelling. Filling a field only proves the record exists; it proves neither that the explanation is right nor that the piece reads well. Where material is missing, say so honestly rather than filling in experience by guesswork.

- Primary material: {the edition, path or source actually read; what of the book was read and what was not}

- Does the material support the path of changing understanding: {yes / no - whether the earlier understanding, the pressure and the change are grounded; with thin material, do not upgrade to a complete breakdown}

## How the book holds together

Record the core understanding, how the parts relate and the author's concern once each here; do not rewrite the same general statement across several fields. For a multi-thread work, describe what is actually there, allowing parallel threads, contrasts, or contradictions that cannot be unified.

- What kind or form of book this is: 
- Starting point, main change and endpoint: {the concrete object or person, the important process, the ending and what is unresolved}
- The core understanding: {how it was understood at first; what forces an extension or a change; how it is seen after the revision}
- How the parts connect: {the indispensable threads and what each does; dependence, division of work, causation, contrast or limitation - a shared topic is not enough}
- What the author most wants to correct or preserve: {inferred from what the book keeps, drops and develops, with the grounds given; invent no authorial psychology}
- Whole-book anchors that must appear in the body: {specific phrases used in the body and grounded in the material; at least 3 for a complete breakdown and 1 for a partial one, separated by |; a higher count does not prove completeness}

## Which threads the body follows

Each row records one question, person, relation or model that keeps developing. One thread may pass through several stages and do different jobs; do not build a separate example for every concept. Where a switch is genuinely necessary, say which concern it picks up and what it adds - there is no need to cling to one object and no limit on the number of threads.

- [thread] Name: | Starting point: | Key advance: | Change before and after: | Reason for the switch: | Grounds and boundary: 

"Change before and after" states the actual judgment, condition or meaning before and after; where there is no outcome, invent none. Keep invented scenarios and the book's facts apart in "Grounds and boundary". The body develops these relations rather than announcing the fields.

## Artifact reading check

Give the evaluator the artifact first, then write their actual answers here; never hand them the answers below. Fidelity to the source is checked separately against the original. Record the specific reading positions and results, not just PASS.

- Whole-book paraphrase and reconstruction of relations: {the genre, the object, the starting point, change and endpoint, the necessary threads and their division of work, the open questions; the shared explanation and its transfer; the developments in the body that support the paraphrase - an overview paragraph copied out is not enough; the evaluator and the scope of the check}
- Reading breaks and revisions: {where a relation is missing, the object changes too early, a name arrives too early, or the text retreats into a blurb or a note on the arrangement; the substantive necessity of the switch; which two paragraphs show the actual change in understanding; how it was revised and the result of rereading; when nothing is found, record the grounds}
- With the final section covered, is the author's concern understandable: {which developments in the body already make the concern visible; whether the final section smuggles in a new lesson or merely repeats the close}
- Which two widely separated turns in the body jointly support it: {"it" means the meaning of the final section; name the two and what they jointly do, without writing a second statement of the book's spirit}
